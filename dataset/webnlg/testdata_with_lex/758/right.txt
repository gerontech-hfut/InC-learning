Bakso is a traditional dish throughout Indonesia and is popular in neighbouring Southeast Asian countries too. Ground beef, tapioca, noodle, rice vermicelli, beef broth, kailan, celery, salted vegetables, and fried shallots are main ingredients in Bakso.
