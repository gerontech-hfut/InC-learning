Bandeja paisa is part of Colombian cuisine that originates from the Paisa region. Main ingredients are kidney beans, red beans, pork belly, white rice, ground meat, chicharon, fried egg, plantain (patacones), chorizo, arepa, hogao sauce, black pudding (morcilla), avocado and lemon.
