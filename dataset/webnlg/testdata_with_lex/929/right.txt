The Baku Turkish Martyrs' Memorial (designed by Huseyin Butuner and Hilmi Guner) is located in Baku, Azerbaijan. It is made from red granite and white marble. It is dedicated to the Ottoman Army Soldiers killed in the Battle of Baku. The native name for the memorial is Türk Şehitleri Anıtı. The leader of Azerbaijan is Artur Rasizade.
