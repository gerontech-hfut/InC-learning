The main ingredients of bakewell pudding are ground almond, jam, butter and eggs. The dish was created at the Rutland Arms, Bakewell, Derbyshire.
Bakewell pudding was created in Rutland Arms, Bakewell in the Derbyshire Dales in 1820. It includes ground almonds, jam, butter, eggs.
