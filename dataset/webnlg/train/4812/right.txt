Addis Ababa in Ethiopia (lead by Hailemariam Desalegn) is the location of Addis Ababa City Hall and Addis Ababa Stadium.
Both Addis Ababa Stadium and City Hall are located in Addis Ababa, Ethiopa, whose leader is Hailemariam Desalegn.
