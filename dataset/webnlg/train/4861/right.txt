Baymax, the Big Hero 6 character created by Steve T Seagle and Duncan Rouleau, is voiced by Scott Adsit.
Duncan Rouleau and Steven T. Seagle are both creators of the character Baymax which featured in the film Big Hero 6. The voice of this character in the film was provided by Scott Adsit.
