Lemper is a variation of Arem arem, which if found nationwide in Indonesia but is more specific to Java. Its main ingredients are compressed rice, which is cooked in banana leaf with vegetables or minced meat fillings.
Arem-arem is made with compressed rice cooked in banana leaf with vegetables or minced meat fillings. It is found all over Indonesia and more specific to Java. Lemper is a variation of that dish.
