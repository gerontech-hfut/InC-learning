The bacon sandwich. which is found in the UK, has different names including: Bacon butty, bacon sarnie, rasher sandwich, bacon sanger, piece 'n bacon, bacon cob, bacon barm and bacon muffin. Bread is an ingredient of this sandwich, which is a variation on a BLT.
