Bakewell tart is made from shortcrust pastry and is a variation of Bakewell pudding. Both are from the Derbyshire Dales where Patrick McLoughlin is a leader.
Bakewell pudding (also known as a Bakewell pudding) originates from the Derbyshire Dales where Patrick McLoughlin is the leader. Shortcrust pastry is a main ingredient.
