Bandeja paisa originates from the Paisa region and part of the Colombian cuisine.The main ingredients of he food are red beans, pork belly, white rice, ground meat, chicharon, fried egg, plantain (patacones), chorizo, arepa, hogao sauce, black pudding (morcilla), avocado and lemon.
