Bandeja paisa comes from the Paisa region and part of the Colombian cuisine.The main ingredients of the food are: red beans, pork belly, white rice, ground meat, chicharon, fried egg, plantain (patacones), chorizo, arepa, hogao sauce, black pudding (morcilla), avocado and lemon.
