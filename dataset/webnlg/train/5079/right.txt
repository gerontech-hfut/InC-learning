Beef kway teow is found in the countries of Indonesia and Singapore. Kway teow, beef tender loin, gula Melaka, sliced, dried black beans, garlic, dark soy sauce, palm sugar, lengkuas, oyster sauce, soya sauce, chilli and sesame oil are main ingredients in beef kway teow.
