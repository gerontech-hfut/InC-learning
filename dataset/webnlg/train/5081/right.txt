The Indonesian dish Beef kway teow is popular nationwide in Singapore and Indonesia. Its ingredients include kway teow, beef tenderloin, gula Melaka, sliced, dried black beans, garlic, dark soy sauce, lengkuas, oyster sauce, soya sauce, chilli and sesame oil.
