Beef kway teow is a dish popular nationwide in Singapore and Indonesia. The main ingredients for Beef Kway Teow are: Kway teow, beef tender loin, gula Melaka, sliced, dried black beans, garlic, dark soy sauce, lengkuas, oyster sauce, soya sauce, chilli and sesame oil.
