Beef kway teow is a dish found in Indonesia and Singapore. Its main ingredients are Kway teow, beef tender loin, gula Melaka, sliced dried black beans, garlic, dark soy sauce, lengkuas, oyster sauce, soya sauce, chilli and sesame oil.
