Bakewell Tart (or pudding) is a dish that comes from the Derbyshire Dales region where Patrick McLoughlin is a leader. Fruit preserves (jam) are an ingredient.
Bakewell tarts contain fruit preserves and are a version of Bakewell pudding. The tart hails from, Patrick McLoughlin led, Derbyshire Dales.
