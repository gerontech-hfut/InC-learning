The Ataturk Monument (Izmir) is located in Turkey and was inaugurated on 1932-07-27. It was designed by Pietro Canonica and is made of bronze.
Pietro Canonica designed the bronze Ataturk monument which was inaugurated in Izmir, Turkey on 27 July 1932.
