The Baku Turkish Martyrs Memorial (known locally as Türk Şehitleri Anıtı) has been dedicated to Ottoman Army soldiers killed in the Battle of Baku. It was designed by Hüseyin Bütüner and Hilmi Güner and is located in Baku, Azerbaijan.
