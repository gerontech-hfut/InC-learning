Michele Marcolini, who was manager of AC Lumezzane, played football for Torino FC Youths, A.C. Chievo Verona and Atalanta BC.
Michele Marcolini is the manager of AC Lumezzane. Previously he played for Atalanta BC and youth football for Torino FC. His own club is AC Chievo Verona.
Michele Marcolini is part of the Atlanta B.C. Club and is affiliated with the youth club Torino F.C.. In addition he plays for A.C. Chievo Verona and manages A.C. Lumezzane.
