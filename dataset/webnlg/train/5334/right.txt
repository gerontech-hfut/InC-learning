Ajax Youth Academy and Jon Ajax are tenants of Sportpark De Toekomst. It is owned by AFC Ajax NV and is the homeground of AFC Ajax (amateurs).
AFC Ajax (amateur)'s ground is Sportpark De Toekomst which is owned by AFC Ajax N.V.. Current tenants of the ground are Jong Ajax and Ajax Youth Academy.
