Agremiação Sportiva Arapiraquense (their full name) play in the Campeonato Brasileiro Série C league which is based in Brazil. Vila Nova Futebol Clube are the champions of this league.
Vila Nova Futebol Clube have been champions of Campeonato Brasileiro Série C. in Brazil in which Agremiação Sportiva Arapiraquense also play. Their full name is "Agremiação Sportiva Arapiraquense".
