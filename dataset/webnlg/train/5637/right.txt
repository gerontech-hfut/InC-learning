The book Wolf Solent is first in a series of books written by John Cowper Powys. The sequel to this books is A Glastonbury Romance which was then followed by Weymouth Sands and then Maiden Castle.
The novels Maiden Castle followed the novel Weymouth Sands, which itself was a sequel to A Glastonbury Romance which followed Wolf Solent. All were written by John Cowper Powys.
Maiden Castle (novel) was preceded by Weymouth Sands. The book "A Glastonbury Romance" was followed by "Weymouth Sands", preceded By Wolf Solent and was written by John Cowper Powys.
