The architecture style of Adisham Hall is Tudor and Jacabian. It was constructed from 1927 to 1931 and is located on St. Benedict's Monastery in Haputale, Sri Lanka.
Adisham Hall is located at St Benedict's Monastery, Adisham, Haputale, Sri Lanka. Work on Adisham Hall, in the architectural style of "Tudor and Jacobean", began in 1927 and finished in 1931.
