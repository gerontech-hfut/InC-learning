Bacon Sandwich, a variation of BLT, where the main ingredients are bread, bacon and a condiment, comes from the United Kingdom, and has different names including bacon butty, bacon sarnie, rasher sandwich, bacong sanger, piece 'n bacon, bacon cob bacon barm and bacon muffin.
The bacon sandwich is a dish from the United Kingdom, also known as a bacon butty or sarnie. It is a variation of the BLT. It usually includes a condiment such as ketchup or brown sauce.
