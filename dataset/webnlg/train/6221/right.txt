Batagor, Siomay & shumai come from Indonesia .Batagor & Siomay are variations of the same dish & Batagor is a variation of shumai. Batagor's main ingredients are fried fish dumplings with tofu & vegetables in peanut sauce.
Batagor is a a dish from the country Indonesia. Other variations of the dish include Shumai and Siomay. The main ingredients are peanut sauce, fired fish dumplings, tofu and vegetables.
