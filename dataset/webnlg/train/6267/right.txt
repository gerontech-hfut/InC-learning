Bionico; can be varied by using cottage cheese, is served at the dessert course, and is found Guadalajara, Mexico (where Silvano Aureoles Conejo is the leader).
Mexico, led by Silvano Aureoles Conejo, is the home of a dessert called bionico. Hailing from the Guadalajara region, bionico variations include cottage cheese.
Bionico is a dish from Guadalajara , Mexico where the leader is Silvano Aureoles Conejo. It is served as a dessert and can sometimes include cottage cheese.
