The 14th New Jersey Volunteer Infantry Monument is located on the Monocacy National Battlefield, Frederick, Maryland. The monument was established on 1907-07-11 and belongs to the category of Historic districts in the United States.
The 14th New Jersey Volunteer Infantry Monument was established July 11th, 1907 and is an historic district founded in the Monocacy National Battlefield. The nearest city is Frederick, Maryland.
