Baku Turkish Martyrs' Memorial, designed by Hüseyin Bütüner and Hilmi Güner, is dedicated to the Ottoman Army soldiers killed in the Battle of Baku. The memorial is in Azerbaijan, whose capital is Baku and whose leader is Artur Rasizade.
