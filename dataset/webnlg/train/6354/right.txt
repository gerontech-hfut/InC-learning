A red granite and white marble monument dedicated to the Ottoman Army soldiers killed in the battle of Baku, is found in the capital of Azerbaijan, Baku. It is called the Turkish Martyrs Memorial and was designed by Huseyin Butuner and Hilmi Guner.
The Baku Turkish Martyrs Memorial is made from red granite and white marble and is dedicated to the Ottoman Army soldiers killed in the Battle of Baku. It is located in Azerbaijan (capital Baku) and was designed by Huseyin Butuner and Hilmi Guner.
