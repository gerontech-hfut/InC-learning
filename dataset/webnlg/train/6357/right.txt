Huseyin Butuner and Hilmi Guner designed the red granite and white marble Baku Turkish Martyrs memorial which is dedicated to the Ottoman army soldiers killed in the Battle of Baku. The memorial is located in Azerbaijan where the leader is Artur Rasizade.
