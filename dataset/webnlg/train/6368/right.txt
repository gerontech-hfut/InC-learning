Frederick County, Maryland is the location of the Monocacy National Battlefield where the 14th New Jersey Volunteer Infantry monument is located. It was established on 11 July 1907 being provided by the National Park Service and is categorised as a historic district in the United States.
The 14th New Jersey Volunteer Infantry Monument which was established 1907-07-11 is found in the Monocacy National Battlefield, Frederick, Maryland. The monument has been provided by the National Park Service and belongs to the category of Historic districts in the United States.
