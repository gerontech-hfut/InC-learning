The 14th New Jersey Volunteer Infantry Monument established 11th July 1907 is located on the Monocacy National Battlefield, Frederick County, Maryland. The National Park Service is responsible for it's upkeep.
The 14th New Jersey Volunteer Infantry Monument by the National Park Service, located in the Monocacy National Battlefield was established in 1907-07-11.The Monocacy National Battlefield is located in Frederick County, Maryland and the nearest city to it is Frederick, Maryland.
