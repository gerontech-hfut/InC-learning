After DeMarce short stories were published in the Grantville Gazettes, there came 1634: The Bavarian Crisis written by Eric Flint of Burbank California, who was influenced by Robert A, Heinlein, as a sequel to Grantville Gazette III.
Eric Flint was born in Burbank, California and is the author of 1634: The Bavarian Crisis. He was influenced by Robert A. Heinlein and has also written DeMarce short stories in the The Grantville Gazettes and Grantville Gazette III.
