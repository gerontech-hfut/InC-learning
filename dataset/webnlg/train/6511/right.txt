Acta Palaeontologica Polonica was published by the Polish Academy of Sciences and is abbreviated to Acta Palaeontol. Pol.. It is identified by the ISSN number of "1732-2421" and the LCCN number of 60040714. It also has the CODEN code of "APGPAC".
