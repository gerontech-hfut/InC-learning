The Apollo 8 is operated by Nasa, its commander was Frank Borman, its backup pilot was Buzz Aldrin and it was crewed by William Anders. William Anders graduated from AFIT in 1962 with an M.S. and retired Sept 1st 1969.
In 1962, William Anders graduated with a M.S. from his alma Mater, AFIT. He became a crew member on Apollo 8, a NASA operated mission. His commander on Apollo 8 was Frank Borman, and the mission's backup pilot was Buzz Aldrin. William Anders retired on the 1st of September, 1969.
