The 11th Mississippi Monument is located in Adams County, Pennsylvania. It was established in 2000 and it is categorized as a Contributing Property. Adams County has Cumberland County (Pa) to its north; Maryland's Frederick County to its north east and Carroll County, Maryland to its southeast.
