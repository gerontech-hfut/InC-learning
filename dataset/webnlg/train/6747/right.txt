The Acharya Institute of Technology is located in Karnatka (which has Telangana to its northeast). It was given the "Technical Campus" status by the All India Council for Technical Education (located in Mumbai). The institute's offered sport is Tennis; the governing body for tennis is the International Tennis Federation.
