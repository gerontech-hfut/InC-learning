Retired American test pilot Alan Bean, born in Wheeler, Texas was a member of the Apollo 12 crew who spent 100305 minutes in space. His Alma Mater is UT Austin, B.S. 1955.
Alan Bean is an American who was born in Wheeler, Texas. He graduated from UT Austin in 1955 with a BS and became a test pilot. He spent a total of 100305 minutes in space whilst serving as a crew member on Apollo 12. He Has now retired.
