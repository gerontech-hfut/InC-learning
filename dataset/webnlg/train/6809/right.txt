Edwin E. Aldrin Jr, also known as Buzz Aldrin, was born on Jan 20, 1938 in Glen Ridge, New Jersey. He graduated from MIT with an Sc.D in 1963. He was a fighter pilot and a member of Apollo 11. He is now retired.
