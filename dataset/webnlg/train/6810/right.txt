Buzz Aldrin was born on Jan 20, 1930 in Glen Ridge, New Jersey. His real name is Edwin Eugene Aldrin Jr. and he is American. Aldrin graduated from MIT in 1963 with a doctorate in Science. He worked as a fighter pilot and has since retired.
