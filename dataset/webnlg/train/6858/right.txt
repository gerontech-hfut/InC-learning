Crew members of NASA's Apollo 8 included William Anders and Frank Borman with Buzz Aldrin as backup pilot. William Anders was born in British Hong Kong, graduated from AFIT, M.S. in 1962 and was selected by NASA in 1963.
William Anders was born in British Hong Kong and graduated from AFIT in 1962 with an M.S. He joined NASA in 1963 and was a member of the crew of NASA operated Apollo 8, which also included Frank Borman and Buzz Aldrin (back up pilot).
