Baku Turkish Martyrs' Memorial, made from red granite and white marble, is dedicated to the Ottoman Army soldiers killed in the Battle of Baku, Azerbaijan. The memorial (native name "Türk Sehitleri Aniti") was designed by Huseyin Butuner and Hilmi Guner. Artur Rasizade is the leader (official title President) of Azerbaijan.
