The Baku Turkish Martyrs' Memorial (known as Turk Sehitleri Aniti) was designed by Hüseyin Bütüner and Hilmi Güner in red granite and white marble, and is dedicated to the Ottoman Army soldiers killed in the Battle of Baku. It is located in Azerbaijan, where the leader is Artur Rasizade, and the National Assembly is the legislative branch of government.
