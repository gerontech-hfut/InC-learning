The Atatürk Monument in İzmir is a bronze monument designed by Pietro Canonica, and inaugurated on July 27th, 1932. It is located in Turkey, where the capital city is Ankara, the currency is the Turkish lira, and the leader is named Ahmet Davutoğlu.
