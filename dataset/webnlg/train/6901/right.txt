In the year 2000 the Acharya Institute of Technology was established in the state of Karnataka, India. The current campus address is "In Soldevanahalli, Acharya Dr. Sarvapalli Radhakrishnan Road, Hessarghatta Main Road, Bangalore – 560090." The Director is Dr G P Prabhukumar and it is affiliated with the Visvesvaraya Technological University.
