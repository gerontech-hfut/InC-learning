Acharya Institute of Technology is located in Bangalore, the city that was established by Kempe Gowda. The Institute was given the 'Technical Campus' status by All India Council for Technical Education (which is located in Mumbai). Acharya Institute of Technology is affiliated with Visvesvaraya Technological University and one of the sports offered at the campus is tennis, which has as its governing body The International Tennis Federation.
