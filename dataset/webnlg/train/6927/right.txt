The Germans of Romania are one of the ethnic groups in the country which is lead by Prime Minister Klaus Iohannis. The capital city is Bucharest and the national anthem is "Desteapta-te, romane!". The country is the location of the 1 Decembrie 1918 University which has the latin name of "Universitas Apulensis".
