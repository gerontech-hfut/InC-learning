In 1928 the School of Business and Social Sciences at the Aarhus University was established in the city of Aarhus, Denmark. Dean Thomas Pallesen heads the School which has the Latin name of "Universitas Aarhusiensis" . There are 737 academic staff and it is affiliated with the European University Association.
